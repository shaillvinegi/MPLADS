ℹ️ ML Anomaly Screening Disclaimer

The Isolation Forest machine learning model generates an Anomaly Risk Score (0–100) based on statistical feature vectors (budget variance, lead times, disbursement ratios, payment tranches, description length). Anomaly Risk Score (0–100) is a normalized statistical screening score. It is NOT a probability of fraud, corruption, or wrongdoing. Potential anomaly indicates that the project's feature pattern differs statistically from other projects in the dataset. It is a screening signal, not proof of wrongdoing.

The supervised Random Forest model estimates the Predicted Delay Probability (0–100%) based on sanction outlay magnitude, administrative approval lead times, and early 90-day fund mobilization velocity. Predicted delay probability is an operational scheduling estimate to help MPs and District Authorities prioritize proactive project monitoring. It does NOT detect or imply contractor negligence, fraud, or administrative corruption.
